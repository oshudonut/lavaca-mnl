The Admin Handbook

Everything you need to run orders, products, the pickup calendar, and site announcements from the admin panel. No tech background required. Work through it once top to bottom, then keep it open in a tab for reference.

New in October 2026: pickup only

Lavaca no longer delivers. Customers now pick a pickup date and a pickup time (any hour from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, any day of the week, same-day included), choose Ready to Serve (heated, ready on pickup) or Frozen for Later (vacuum-packed, with reheating instructions) for each item, and add a required Special Request. Customers also give their address (house/lot and street, village, city, ZIP) and can add their Instagram name. Phone numbers and emails are checked before an order goes through. There are no more AM/PM slots or order limits. Payment details and the GCash QR code can be changed from Settings, and the closure banner shows only the message you type.

Signing in

The admin panel is a separate, private area of the Lavaca MNL website — customers never see it.

  1. Open the admin sign-in page on your phone or computer's browser.
  2. Type your email and password into the two boxes.
  3. Click Sign In. If the details don't match, the form will say "Invalid email or password" — check for typos (especially extra spaces) and try again.
  4. You'll land on the Dashboard automatically once you're in.

Orders

Every order ever placed, newest first. This is where most of your day-to-day work happens.

  1. Click Orders in the top bar.
  2. Type in the search box to find an order by order number or customer name Use the buttons under it to show only one kind of order, like To review (payments waiting on you) or Waiting for payment.
  3. Each order shows the pickup day and time, customer, items, order number, status and total. On a phone each order is a card. Tap any order to open it.
  4. To remove orders in bulk: tick the box next to each row you want to delete (or the box in the header to select everything showing), click Delete … selected, then confirm.
    This can't be undone

    Deleting an order erases it completely — no record is kept. For a normal cancellation, open the order and use Cancel this order instead (see next section) — it notifies the customer and keeps a record.

Good to know

Orders where the customer doesn't upload a payment screenshot within 2 hours expire automatically, and the customer gets an email about it. You don't need to do anything for that.

Working an order

Clicking into an order number opens this screen — customer info, pickup details, the special request, items, the payment proof, and the buttons that move the order forward.

Customer
Name, email, phone, the customer's special request, their address, their Instagram name if they gave one, and whether they're paying by GCash or bank transfer. Read the special request before preparing the order.
Pickup
The date and the pickup time the customer chose, for example 2:00 PM. Older orders from before October 2026 show their old delivery window instead.
Items
Every product ordered, its weight, whether the customer wants it Ready to Serve or Frozen for Later, the quantity, and the line total, plus the grand total. The Orders list shows this choice next to each item too.
Payment screenshot
Once payment has been submitted, the photo (or PDF) the customer uploaded as proof appears here. Click it to view full size — the link expires after an hour, so if it stops opening, just refresh the page.

On an order waiting for review, “Check the payment” comes first

Accept payment Reject payment
  1. Accept payment: at the top while the order is To review. Check the screenshot against the amount due, then tap Accept payment. The customer is emailed and the order becomes Confirmed.
  2. Reject payment: if the photo is blurry, the amount is wrong, or it went to the wrong account. You’ll be asked to confirm, and you can add a short reason for the customer (optional). They’re emailed to upload a new screenshot.
  3. Cancel this order: under Other actions at the bottom, any time before the order is completed. Write a reason and confirm. The customer is emailed and the order is kept on record.
  4. Delete order: also under Other actions. It erases the order completely without telling the customer. Use it only for test orders, duplicates or spam.

Products

The products customers can choose on the order page. Add new items, edit prices, or take something off the menu.

  1. Click Products in the top bar.
  2. To add something new, click + Add product, fill in the name, size, price, a short product code and a description, then click Add product.
  3. To change an existing product, click Edit on its card, change what you need, then click Save changes.
  4. To temporarily hide a product without losing its data — say, it's sold out — untick Available to customers on its card. It then says "Hidden from customers" until you tick it again.
  5. To permanently remove a product, click Delete on its card, then Yes, delete.

  1. Click Calendar in the top bar, then use the arrows beside the month name to move between months.
  2. If a whole month hasn't been set up yet, click Open all days this month to open every day in that month, Mondays included, then close or adjust any date that needs to be different. Days you've already set up are left as they are, so it's safe to click again to fill in missing days.
  3. Click any date to open its panel (on a phone, it slides up from the bottom).
    • Set the day to Open or Closed using the toggle at the top.
    • If Open: under Products available, untick anything that shouldn't be orderable that specific day (e.g. one item sold out).
    • If Closed: pick a closure type — Operational, Holiday, or Vacation — and optionally jot down why, for your own records.
  4. Click Save.
Pickup times

On any open date, customers choose a pickup time from a dropdown: 9:00 AM, 10:00 AM, and so on up to 6:00 PM. Same-day orders are allowed: customers can book any time that hasn't passed yet, so earlier times today show as unavailable. To stop orders for a day, close that date.

Settings

Two things live here: the payment details customers pay into, and the closure banner shown when Lavaca isn't taking orders.

Payment details
The GCash number and account name, and the BPI and BDO account numbers and names, that customers pay into. Change any field and click Save payment details. Customers see the new details on the payment page from then on, so double-check numbers before saving.
GCash QR code
Under the GCash fields, click Upload QR code and pick the QR image (JPG or PNG, up to 2MB). It appears on the payment page right away, above the GCash number; there's no need to click Save. Use Replace QR code to swap it, or Remove to take it down.

Closure announcement

  1. Under Are you taking orders?, choose No, closed for now to replace the order page with your message, so customers can't place orders. Choose Yes, open for orders to take orders again.
  2. Write the banner message. It's required while the banner is on, and it's the only text customers see, so include anything they need to know, such as when you'll reopen.
  3. Fill in Closed from, and Reopens on if you know the date. These dates are for your records and aren't shown to customers. The banner stays up until you switch back to Yes, open for orders.
  4. Check What customers will see, then click Save.

Signing out

Click Sign Out in the top-right corner of any admin page, any time. It's a good habit if you're using a shared or public computer.

Quick reference: order statuses

The colored label on every order tells you exactly where it stands. Here's what each one means.

Waiting for paymentOrder placed, but the customer hasn't paid or uploaded proof yet.
To reviewA payment screenshot is in — waiting on you to Confirm or Reject it.
ConfirmedPayment approved. Prepare the order for the customer's pickup date and time.
CancelledCalled off by you or the customer, with a reason on file.
ExpiredNo payment screenshot within 2 hours. Closed automatically, no action needed.
About the Confirmed filter

The Confirmed filter on the Orders page also includes older statuses (Ready for pickup, Completed). Nothing in the panel moves new orders into those, so paid orders stay Confirmed.

If something looks off

Page looks empty or stale
Refresh the browser page — the admin panel always loads the latest data fresh, it just needs a reload after certain actions.
Can't sign in
Double-check the email and password for typos. If the password's genuinely lost, ask the developer to reset it.
Payment screenshot won't open
Its link expires after an hour for security — refresh the order page to get a fresh one.
